Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how TransLay collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Google Workspace™ add-on.

1. What we collect

When you use TransLay, we process:

  • Google account identity — your email address and basic profile information, used for authentication and billing.
  • Presentation content — the text and structure of the slides you choose to translate. This is sent to our LLM translation provider for processing.
  • Usage data — slide counts, translation timestamps, and Task IDs, used to enforce quotas and provide support.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment processor (Paddle). We never store full card numbers.

2. How we use it

We use the information we collect to operate the translation service, enforce subscription limits, respond to support requests, and improve reliability. We do not sell your data. We do not use your presentation content to train models.

3. Data retention

Presentation content is processed transiently to complete the translation request and is not persistently stored in our databases, Redis, or application logs after processing finishes. Account metadata, usage records, and billing records are retained for as long as needed to operate the service and satisfy legal obligations.

4. Third-party processors

TransLay uses the following subprocessors: Google Workspace™ (add-on hosting), language model and cloud infrastructure providers required to execute translations, and Paddle (payments). Each processor is engaged only to the extent needed to operate the service.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@example.com. If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR, UK DPA, and CCPA respectively — we will honor all valid requests.

6. Security

We use TLS in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and principle-of-least-privilege access controls. Despite our efforts, no system is 100% secure; please report vulnerabilities to security@example.com.

7. Children

TransLay is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via the landing page with at least 30 days' notice.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@example.com.
